04 Technical FAQ

How can we help?

Have other questions? Reach out to us and we'll help route the job.

Contact us
What is the lead time for getting parts delivered?

Parts can be delivered via Uber as soon as same day in the San Francisco Bay Area. Outside the Bay Area, you select shipping speed at checkout; the shortest lead time is 2 days.

What manufacturing processes do you offer?

3-axis CNC, millturn (combined milling and turning), and 5-axis CNC for complex parts. Upload a STEP or STP file to get a quote.

What design files do you accept?

STEP and STP files.

What is the minimum order quantity?

One part.

Do you ship outside the Bay Area?

Yes, nationwide across the United States. You select shipping speed at checkout, and the fastest option is 2-day shipping. Bay Area customers can also choose same-day Uber delivery or pickup in Berkeley.
